The player wants to know how to get the highest xp rates per hour in the Volcanic Mine.
The Volcanic mine is a game in which the player mines a boulder for xp while trying to subdue the Volcano.
First there is 30 seconds waiting for the game to begin.
The game then begins with 10 minutes on the clock and a stability of 50. If the stability hits 0, the volcano blows up. The stability caps out at 100.

Stability logic:
There are 3 chambers, A, B and C. They are either blocked or unblocked. The player can block/unblock them by going up to them and 'capping'.
There are 3 vents, A,B and C, and they have a status, which is a % between 0 and 100. When their corresponding chamber is blocked, the status goes up. When unblocked, it goes down.
At the start of the game, they have a random status between  [30, 70]
The status of a vent is unknown until the player checks it.
The status of the vent updates every 6 seconds during the game. This is the rules for vent updates:
  Update order:
        1. C
        2. B
        3. A

    Rules:
        - All vents move 2 by default.
        - A vent in the 41-59 range moves 1 instead.
        - If A is in 41-59, B and C each move 1 less.
        - If B is in 41-59, C moves 1 less.
        - Movement is floored at 0.
Every 15 seconds, the stability updates. It is based on the vent statuses, with the below logic:
def vent_score(value: int) -> int:
    return math.ceil((50 - abs(50 - value)) / 3)

stability += -25 +  vent_score(A) +  vent_score(B) +  vent_score(C)

After the 5 minute mark, the chamber statuses 'reset', becoming a new random number between [25, 75], and the player will need to check the vent in order to see each chambers value again. The mine stability does not reset however.
The last stability update occurs when there is 45 seconds left on the clock. After that, the mine will not blow up.

To keep the mine from blowing up, the player should cap the chambers to improve the vent status, in order to keep the volcano stability above 0.

Mining and xp:
The main source of xp in Volcanic Mine is mining the boulder. It starts in position 1 with 41 health. Every succesful mine deducts a health. After 41 succesful mines, it moves to position 2.
This continues up to position 5, where it instead has 61 health instead.
Mining it gives xp and points. See below for average points, xp, and time for each boulder.

Capping gives 50 points. Player can cap up to 6 times per game.
Checking vents gives 50 points per game. As there are 3 vents and they reset at 5 minute mark, thats a max of 6 vent checks possible.
After completely mining boulder 5, a bonus 100 points is rewarded.
Points are turned into xp after the player leaves the mine,at 3.895xp per point.
